# 🍽️ Swiggy Sales Analysis | SQL & Power BI Dashboard

## 1. Project Overview

**Swiggy Sales Analysis** is a data analytics project designed to analyze sales performance, customer ratings, order trends, and restaurant performance using SQL and Power BI.

The project transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights through data analysis and interactive visualizations. It helps understand sales trends, customer preferences, order patterns, and overall business performance.

## 2. Project Objective / Purpose

The primary objective of this project is to analyze Swiggy's sales and operational performance and identify meaningful patterns in customer ordering behavior.

The analysis focuses on answering important business questions:

- How are sales performing over time?
- Which states and cities contribute the most to sales?
- What are the ordering trends across different periods?
- How do Veg and Non-Veg categories contribute to sales?
- What is the average order value?
- How do customer ratings reflect restaurant performance?
- Which restaurants and categories perform better?

## 3. Tech Stack

The project uses the following technologies:

- **SQL:** Data querying, aggregation, filtering, grouping, and analytical calculations.
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Power Query:** Data transformation and preparation.
- **DAX:** Calculated measures and KPI development.

## 4. Dataset / Data Source

The project uses Swiggy-related sales and order data containing information about sales, orders, restaurants, locations, food categories, and customer ratings.

Key analytical dimensions include:
- Order information
- Restaurant details
- Geographic information
- Food categories
- Customer ratings
- Sales and revenue information

## 5. Key Features / Analysis

### Business Problem
Understanding sales performance and customer ordering behavior is important for identifying business opportunities and improving operational decisions.

This project analyzes sales, order volumes, restaurant performance, and customer ratings to provide a consolidated view of business performance.

### Dashboard KPIs

- Total Sales: ₹53.01M
- Total Orders: 197.43K
- Average Order Value: ₹268.51
- Total Rating Count: 5.59M

### Key Visualizations

- **Sales Trend Analysis:** Monthly, weekly, and daily sales trends.
- **Quarterly Performance:** Comparison of sales, ratings, and orders across quarters.
- **Geographical Analysis:** State-wise sales and top-performing cities.
- **Category Analysis:** Comparison of Veg and Non-Veg sales performance.
- **Customer Rating Analysis:** Understanding customer ratings and restaurant performance.
- **Interactive Dashboard:** Dynamic filters and slicers for Month, Category, and Restaurant.

## 6. Business Impact & Insights

The dashboard helps stakeholders:

- Identify high-performing states and cities.
- Understand monthly and quarterly sales patterns.
- Analyze customer ordering preferences.
- Compare Veg and Non-Veg category performance.
- Monitor average order value and order volume.
- Evaluate restaurant performance through ratings.
- Support data-driven business decisions.
